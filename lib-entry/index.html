Rate structure

Dual rates are not entered on this screen
A dual rate charges on-site and off-site work at two different rates, and a rate that changes part-way through the contract has to be weighted by the months on each side of the change. Neither calculation is built here — a dual-rate budget is worked out with your contracting officer before the LIB is entered.

What this rate is applied to

A subtotal counts toward the indirect base only while it is checked. Uncheck one and watch the base and the indirect dollars below drop by that amount.
