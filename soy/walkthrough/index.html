SOY future state: Walkthrough

This prototype shows the whole recommended future state of start-of-year (SOY) budgets — screens and behavior together — played through one FY2028 cycle with mock data shaped like FY2027 (about 240 items and $327M baseline). Nothing here changes CBFish; it’s for review and sign-off before anything is built.

How to use it
  • Start a step below: each link loads the demo data for that point in the year, so you can begin anywhere.
  • Viewing as switches between the budget administrator and a project manager (Casey Navarro).
  • Show what’s changing outlines and numbers every change on a screen; List explains each one — what it was before, what it does now, the problem it solves, and its tag.
  • Your clicks are saved in this browser. Reset or Jump to step in the prototype controls at the top of every screen.
  • All names, comments and amounts are invented.

The FY2028 cycle, step by step

  1. 1. The FY2028 cycle drafts itself

    Demo date
    Jan 15, 2027
    Role
    Budget administrator
    • FY2028 appears in All cycles as Draft, with the standard dates.
    • FY2024–2027 are flagged as still open.
    Start at All cycles

  2. 2. Set up the cycle

    Demo date
    Jan 15, 2027
    Role
    Budget administrator
    • Confirm the pre-filled dates.
    • Carry forward 230 items; 7 ended or ineligible items start unchecked, each with its reason.
    • Add 4 new projects.
    • Apply a 2.5% COLA to a filtered subset; the report names the two-year off-year projects and the projects with no baseline.
    Open the setup wizard

  3. 3. EWB proposed

    Demo date
    Mar 1, 2027
    Role
    Budget administrator
    • Select all items and set EWB proposed from baseline — the preview names the stage and the count.
    • The 6 items with no baseline are listed by name; enter them by hand with a rationale in the item panel.
    Open items

  4. 4. Open PM proposed

    Demo date
    Apr 12, 2027
    Role
    Budget administrator, then project manager
    • Open PM proposed from the overview and preview the email PMs receive.
    • As Casey Navarro (PM), land on “5 of your projects need input”, propose an increase with a rationale on one, and agree with EWB on the rest.
    • Fast-forward with “Simulate PM responses”: the automatic reminder goes to the 4 PMs who haven’t responded.
    Open the overview (admin) See it as the PM

  5. 5. Final budget

    Demo date
    May 10, 2027
    Role
    Budget administrator
    • Open Final budget. Decision makers meet outside CBFish.
    • Set finals from PM proposed for agreed items and from EWB where there was no PM input; enter the rest by hand.
    • Notify PMs with a summary of their final amounts and rationale.
    Open the overview Final decisions

  6. 6. Transfers

    Demo date
    Jun 1, 2027
    Role
    Budget administrator
    • Transfer everything under “FY28 SOY – Round 1 – 2027-06-01”.
    • Change one final amount afterwards: a warning appears, a reason is required, and the item shows in the Variance view.
    • A Round 2 transfer reconciles it.
    Open transfers

  7. 7. Close-out

    Demo date
    Jun 15, 2027
    Role
    Budget administrator
    • The checklist is all green; close the cycle. It locks for everyone.
    • All cycles now shows FY2028 Closed, with FY2024–2027 flagged for close-out.
    Open close-out

What each change solves

42 changes in all: 28 need front-end work only (UI), 15 need backend or data work (BE), and 13 show a recommended answer to a decision (DEC). Turn on “Show what’s changing” on any screen to see them in place.

Scope

In scope for sign-off

Every SOY screen and flow in this prototype, for the budget administrator and project manager roles, with the behavior changes they include.

Out of scope

  • Changes to Funds, working budgets or Budget Decision screens beyond what SOY transfers need
  • Moving the decision makers’ process into CBFish (possible later)
  • Sponsor-facing views
  • Data migration beyond closing FY2024–2027
