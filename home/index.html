Columbia Basin Fish & Wildlife Program

An unprecedented view into the region’s fish & wildlife mitigation.

Photo: Rob Meyers / BPA

Welcome to the Columbia Basin Fish & Wildlife Program

Bonneville Power Administration funds work to protect and restore the fish and wildlife affected by hydroelectric dams on the Columbia River and its tributaries. CBFish tracks that work — the projects, the contracts, and what they achieve — across Idaho, Montana, Oregon, and Washington.
